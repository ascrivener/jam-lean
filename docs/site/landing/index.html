jam-lean

A Lean 4 model of JAM from the Gray Paper v0.8.1, used to prove theorems about real compiled service binaries.

  • A Companion to the Gray Paper

    The paper's equations next to the Lean definitions that implement them (read from the model's source), the readings chosen where the paper is ambiguous, and the theorems proved. Pilot chapters: §6 Safrole and Appendix A, the PVM's single step.

  • API reference

    Every definition and theorem of the PVM, JAM and JamCrypto libraries, generated by doc-gen4. Docstrings cite the Gray Paper section and equation.

  • Source on GitHub

    The model, what is proved (docs/whats-proven.md) and the findings (docs/upstream-candidates.md).
